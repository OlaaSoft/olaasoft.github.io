Boxlings — Privacy Policy

Blind Box ASMR · Last updated 19 September 2026

Boxlings is a blind-box collecting game published by OlaaSoft. This policy explains what the app stores and what leaves your device.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your collection, shard balance, progress, streak, achievements and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

2. Online Leaderboard (Optional)

If you use the leaderboard, we store an anonymous player id, the nickname and avatar you choose, the country you select, and your score. Sign-in is anonymous — we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Choosing a nickname is optional; you can play the whole game without ever appearing on a board.

3. Advertising

Boxlings is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, which may use Unity Ads as a mediated ad source. These providers may use your device's advertising identifier to select ads and to measure whether an ad led to an install.

Because Boxlings is suitable for all ages and we cannot tell how old a player is, every ad request is sent with child-directed treatment and is limited to general-audience content. The app shows no app-open ads, and no ad interrupts the opening of a box.

On iOS you are asked for permission before any tracking happens. If you decline, you still see ads — they are simply not matched to your interests. You can change this later in your device settings.

Google's own privacy policy covers what they collect: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. No Purchases

Boxlings contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Shards are earned by playing and cannot be bought. We never collect or process payment information of any kind.

5. Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry to find out when the app crashes. Crash reports are tied to an anonymous identifier, never to you personally; Sentry runs with personal data and screenshot attachment switched off.

Boxlings collects no product analytics. Firebase Analytics is disabled in the app, and we do not track which screens you visit or how you play.

6. Offline Use

The game plays fully offline. A connection is only needed for ads, the leaderboard and crash reporting.

7. Children's Privacy

Boxlings is suitable for all ages and is treated as a mixed-audience app that may be used by children. Because we cannot tell a player's age, we apply the stricter setting to everyone: every ad request carries child-directed treatment, ad content is limited to a general-audience rating, and personalised advertising is not requested.

We never ask for a name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and the game can be played in full without ever appearing on a leaderboard. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Deleting Your Data

Everything stored on your device goes away when you delete the app.

If you have used the leaderboard, you can delete what is stored online from inside the game: Settings → Delete online account. That removes your anonymous account, your leaderboard entries and your public collection. Your offline toys and progress stay on this device. The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, and because sign-in is anonymous there is no way for us to restore it afterwards.

If the app cannot reach our servers midway through, the deletion resumes and finishes the next time it can. You do not need to ask us, and you do not need an account with anyone to do it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
