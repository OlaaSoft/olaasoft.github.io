Wend: Word Path Puzzle

A word path puzzle: trace hidden words through a grid of letters until every cell is used. Free on iPhone, iPad and Android, with unlimited levels.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How to play

  1. Press the first letter of a word and drag through its letters in order.
  2. Move only between touching cells: left, right, up or down.
  3. Paths never cross, and no word passes through a gray wall.
  4. The word slots show how many words are hidden and how long each one is.
  5. Find every word and the whole board is filled: the level is solved.

Full guide with a worked example: wend.olaasoft.com/how-to-play

Help

🌍

Change the word language

Tap the language chip on the game screen, or open Settings, to pick one of 42 word languages.

💡

Stuck on a level

Hint marks where the shortest unfinished word begins. Solve Word fills in a whole word for you.

🔄

Start a board again

Restart Puzzle in the options menu clears the board so you can try a fresh approach.

✈️

Playing offline

Levels are generated on your phone, so you can keep playing without a connection. The leaderboard needs one.
