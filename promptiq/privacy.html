PromptIQ — Privacy Policy

Prompt engineering quizzes · Last updated 23 September 2026

PromptIQ is a quiz app about writing prompts for AI language models, published by OlaaSoft. This policy explains what the app stores and what leaves your device. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no account to create.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your chosen language, sound and haptics preferences are saved locally on your device. Quiz answers and scores are held only for the duration of a quiz and are not written to storage or uploaded. Deleting the app deletes everything it has stored.

2. Advertising

PromptIQ is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, which may use additional mediated ad sources such as Unity Ads and Pangle. These providers may use your device's advertising identifier to select which ads you see and to measure whether an ad led to an install.

Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — a consent form is shown before any ad loads, and the app requests no ads at all if you decline. On iOS, Apple's own tracking permission prompt is shown as part of the same sequence. You can reopen these choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the app.

3. Crash Reporting and Analytics

The app reports crashes and usage through Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, Sentry and Mixpanel. What these receive is a device-level record — device model, operating system version, app version, a crash stack trace where one occurred, and which screens were opened. They do not receive your name, your email address, or the content of anything you type.

4. Notifications and Remote Settings

The app registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send occasional notices, and reads Firebase Remote Config so we can tell you when an update is required without shipping a new version. On both platforms notifications are only delivered if you allow them when asked.

5. No Purchases

This version of PromptIQ sells nothing. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription, so no payment or purchase information is collected. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the feature ships.

6. Offline Use

Quizzes work without a network connection. Without one you will see no ads and no notices, and nothing is sent anywhere.

7. Children's Privacy

PromptIQ is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. If you have a question about anything described here, write to us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version is published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
