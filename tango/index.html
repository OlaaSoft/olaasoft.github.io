Tango

A calm but addictive sun & moon logic puzzle. Fill the 6×6 grid, follow a few simple rules, and play as many boards as you like.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How to play

  1. Tap a cell to switch between a sun and a moon.
  2. No more than two of the same symbol can sit next to each other.
  3. Each row and column must hold an equal number of suns and moons.
  4. Read the clues between cells: = means the same symbol, × means opposite symbols.
  5. Every board has one logical solution — no luck, no guessing.
