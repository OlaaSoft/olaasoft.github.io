Hand and Foot Royale — Privacy Policy

Card game · Last updated 5 November 2026

Hand and Foot Royale is a card game published by OlaaSoft. This policy explains what information the app and its game server process, why, and the choices available to you.

1. Accounts

You can play without registering. The app uses Firebase Authentication (Google) to create an anonymous guest account with a random user ID. You can choose to link your account to Apple or Google where those options are available; the provider then shares your account identifier with Firebase and, if you allow it, your email address and name. We do not receive or store your password.

2. Nickname, Avatar and Online Play

Your nickname and chosen avatar are stored on your device. When you play online, the app sends them to our game server together with your user ID, so that rooms, invitations, friends and reconnection work. Your nickname and avatar are visible to other players at your table and to friends you connect with. Please do not use personal information as your nickname.

The game server processes your moves and game state and, like any internet service, your IP address in order to maintain the connection. It currently keeps rooms and friend lists in memory, and they are lost when the server restarts. The hosting infrastructure may keep standard technical logs.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores your settings, saved games, statistics, achievements, coin balance and consent-related choices on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

4. Advertising and Consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions in order to select, deliver and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and their own policies.

Where consent is required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. On iOS, the app may then ask for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; your advertising identifier is available only if you allow it. Where available, you can change your choices in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings. Remove Ads and VIP turn off ads.

5. Analytics and Crash Reports

After the consent flow allows it, the app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These may process an app-instance identifier, automatic events such as first open and sessions, device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and crash details such as stack traces. Advertising storage and ad personalisation signals are denied for Firebase Analytics.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your card details. Adapty is used to retrieve products and confirm what you have purchased. It processes an anonymous profile identifier, purchase and subscription status, device and operating-system information and IP address. Coins, Remove Ads and VIP status are tied to these records and your device.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account in the app's account settings. This erases the account's local data and deletes its Firebase Authentication identity. Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your Apple or Google account. To ask about any other data, contact us at the address below.

8. Service Providers

We use Google (Firebase, AdMob and its advertising partners), Adapty, Apple and Google Play. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information for money.

9. Children

Hand and Foot Royale is intended for users aged 17 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us so we can remove the related data.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version is published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
