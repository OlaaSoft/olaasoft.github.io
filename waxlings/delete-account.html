Waxlings — Delete online account

Published by Olaasoft MMC.

In the app

  1. Open Waxlings and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete online account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion. Keep the app online until completion. If the request is interrupted, return to the account status screen and retry.

This removes the anonymous Firebase account, public profile, daily and weekly rankings, and shared collection. Local offline game progress remains on your device; clear app data or uninstall to remove it, subject to device backups.

Temporary deletion markers prevent delayed writes from restoring deleted records. A hashed recovery receipt confirms completion without retaining an account identifier after success. These records expire automatically, normally within seven days plus provider cleanup delay.

Without access to the app

Email choicy.appstore@gmail.com with the subject “Waxlings account deletion”. Include the public nickname or game identifier needed to locate the record. Do not send passwords. We may ask for information to verify that you own the account.
