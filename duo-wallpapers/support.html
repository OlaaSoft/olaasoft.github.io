Duo Wallpapers — Support

Find a wallpaper that feels like you

Browse the collection, open a wallpaper and preview it on your screen. No account is required.

Save or apply a wallpaper

Choose Save to Photos to export the image, then set it as wallpaper in iOS. You can also follow the in-app Shortcuts guide. Photos access is add-only; the app does not read your photo library.

One rewarded unlock every 24 hours

Watch an optional rewarded ad to unlock one selected wallpaper. The next free unlock becomes available 24 hours after the completed reward. You can reuse the currently unlocked wallpaper during that time. The allowance is local to this device.

Duo Premium

The optional monthly subscription unlocks unlimited wallpaper exports without rewarded ads while it is active. The purchase screen shows the price for your App Store region. Apple handles payment and renewal.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Open Duo Premium and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple account that made the purchase. To manage or cancel, use Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Troubleshooting

If the catalog or an ad does not load, check your connection and try again. If a required-update screen appears, update the app from the App Store. Advertising consent choices can be reopened from the Premium screen where required.

Contact

Email choicy.appstore@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.
