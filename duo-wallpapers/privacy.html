Duo Wallpapers — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Duo Wallpapers is published by OlaaSoft. This notice describes the native iOS app with optional rewarded advertisements and a monthly subscription. Available features depend on the installed version and service configuration.

No sign-up

You can browse wallpapers without registering. We do not ask for your name, email address or password. No-login use still involves the service data described below. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send.

Wallpaper delivery and device storage

Wallpapers and the catalog are delivered through Cloudflare. Network requests expose technical data such as an IP address, requested resource and request time to the delivery service for operation, security and troubleshooting. The app caches images and catalog information on your device.

Your selected wallpaper and Shortcut completion preference are stored locally. The selected rewarded wallpaper and the next available reward time are stored in the device Keychain to maintain the 24-hour allowance. This local record is not synchronized as a daily allowance between devices and can survive app reinstallation. The app does not upload your photo library. Saving requests add-only Photos permission; iOS Shortcuts can use the selected image when you choose the Shortcut route.

Optional rewarded advertising

When you choose to unlock a wallpaper with an advertisement, Google AdMob delivers the ad. Google's SDK may process IP address (including an approximate location derived from it), app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. We use Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage consent where required.

iOS tracking permission is optional. Denying it does not prevent browsing or subscribing. The app requests non-personalized ads when tracking permission is not granted; non-personalized ads still involve processing necessary for delivery and measurement. Where required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened from the Premium screen. You can also change tracking permission in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and purchase validation

Apple handles billing. We do not receive your payment-card information. Adapty creates an anonymous profile and processes purchase and transaction information, subscription status, technical app/device information and paywall events so the app can validate and restore access. The app disables Adapty's IDFA and IP-address collection options and does not supply an email address or a custom account identifier. This does not prevent network infrastructure from processing connection information.

Use Restore Purchases to recover eligible purchases through your Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

Retention and your choices

Local caches and preferences remain until removed by the app or iOS; Keychain records can remain after uninstalling. Apple, Adapty, Google and Cloudflare retain service records according to their purposes and policies, including transaction, security and legal requirements. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for records controlled by these providers.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us for a request relating to Duo Wallpapers. Without a registered account, we may need information that lets us locate the relevant anonymous record. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Children, changes and contact

The app is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We may update this notice when the app or its providers change, and will update the date above.
